Gradient Descent Explorer

Gradient descent walks downhill: xnew = x − α · f′(x). Pick a function, choose a start point and learning rate α, then step through the iterations.

Tip: click or tap the plot to choose the starting point x₀. Orange dots are the iterations; the dashed line is the tangent (slope) at the current point.

Things to try
  • x²: α = 0.1 converges smoothly; α = 0.9 zig-zags; α > 1 diverges.
  • Quartic: start at x₀ = 2 and you land in the local minimum; start at x₀ = −2 and you reach the global minimum. Start near the local maximum (x ≈ 0.12) and progress is very slow.
  • Small α is safe but slow; large α is fast until it overshoots.
  • Two variables: the contour plot shows f as colour bands (centre = minimum), the white path is the descent, and the yellow dashed segment is the next step −α·∇f. On x₁² + 5x₂², α = 0.18 zig-zags across the valley and α > 0.2 diverges.
